AETHER XO — Tactical Field Guide

1. Classic 3×3 Mode
Traditional Tic-Tac-Toe rules. First player to align 3 marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the round.
2. Infinite 3-Cap (Vanishing Mode)
Each player can only have 3 marks on the grid at once. When you have 3 marks placed, your oldest mark pulses red—placing your 4th mark immediately removes your oldest mark! Draws are impossible; every match ends in a decisive tactical victory.
3. Tactical 4×4 Grid
Expanded 16-cell battleground. Align 4 marks in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
